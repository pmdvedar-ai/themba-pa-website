Free PDF planning guide

Preceptor Accountability Planning Guide

An eight-page operational planning guide for reviewing preceptor roles, training, measurement, recognition, and program infrastructure.

Open the PDF guide (PDF, about 270 KB)

What’s inside

  • A preceptor competency model and tiered proficiency levels
  • An eight-hour modular preceptor-training outline
  • Quarterly evaluation and outcome-measurement prompts
  • Accountability, reassignment, recognition, and incentive considerations
  • A seven-dimension program-infrastructure self-assessment

How to use it

Review the guide with the leaders who own preceptor selection, preparation, feedback, and recognition. Use it to compare current local practices with a more explicit operating structure, then choose one bounded improvement to adapt through your normal governance process.

Planning boundary

This guide is an organization-level planning resource. It does not assess individual clinical competence or readiness, establish clinical policy, replace legal or HR review, or determine employment, credentialing, privileging, or patient-care decisions.
