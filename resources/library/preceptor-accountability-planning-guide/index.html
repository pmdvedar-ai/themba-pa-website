Preceptor Accountability Planning Guide

A seven-page operational planning guide for clarifying preceptor teaching expectations, feedback, review, and program follow-through.

Open the PDF guide (PDF, about 20 KB)

What’s inside

  • A preceptor teaching-practice model and local review prompts
  • A modular preparation curriculum for local education planning
  • Feedback, debriefing, and follow-through prompts
  • Accountability, role-fit, recognition, and governance boundaries for local review
  • A conversation plan and before-adoption checklist

How to use it

Review the guide with the leaders who own preceptor selection, preparation, feedback, and recognition. Use it to compare current local practices with a more explicit operating structure, then choose one bounded improvement to adapt through your normal governance process.

Review before local adoption

Before using the guide to set expectations or make program decisions, have the appropriate clinical education, APP leadership, HR, legal, and governance reviewers adapt the examples for local supervision, escalation, safety, credentialing, privileging, recognition, and role review. The PDF is a planning aid, not a validated standard or a substitute for local policy.

Planning boundary

This guide is an organization-level planning resource. It does not assess individual clinical competence or readiness, establish clinical policy, replace legal or HR review, or determine employment, credentialing, privileging, or patient-care decisions.
