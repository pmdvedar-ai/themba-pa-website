Informal expectations make accountability difficult to sustain

When preceptor responsibilities, decision authority, feedback routines, and escalation paths depend on individual habits, teams can struggle to see who owns the process or what follow-through is still needed. The toolkit connects those operating questions without creating clinical or employment standards.

What the toolkit helps you establish

  • Locally approved preceptor-role expectations and boundaries
  • Explicit ownership, approval, consultation, and communication roles
  • A consistent operational check-in and feedback cadence
  • Visible actions, dependencies, barriers, escalation, and evidence
  • Time-bounded launch and 30-, 60-, and 90-day leadership reviews

How the pieces work together

  1. Frame the rollout. Use the Quick-Start Guide to confirm scope, boundaries, decision authority, and sequence.
  2. Define expectations. Adapt and locally approve the Role Charter.
  3. Make responsibility visible. Assign ownership and decisions in the Matrix.
  4. Run the process. Use the Check-In Template and Tracker to coordinate follow-through.
  5. Review and adapt. Use the Meeting Pack to document decisions and approve improvements.
