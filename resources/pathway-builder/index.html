First-90-Day Pathway Builder

Create a locally defined first-90-day development pathway for new or transitioning surgical APPs. Use this guided tool to organize development stages across the first 30, 60, and 90 days, operational milestones, a designated pathway-owner role, check-ins, and locally maintained documentation.

Designed for lead APPs, APP managers, and surgical-service leaders.

This pathway supports local operational planning for early APP development and team integration. It does not ensure retention or determine whether any individual will remain with a program.
