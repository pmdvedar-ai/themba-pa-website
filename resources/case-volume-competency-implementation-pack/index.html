The problem

Case-volume information is not automatically development visibility

Teams can accumulate case-volume information without a practical system for turning it into a usable review cadence. This pack connects local categories—such as conduit harvest, primary cardiac cases, or postoperative management—reusable logging, period summaries, development review, action planning, and periodic follow-up in one bounded workflow. These examples are prompts for local configuration, not universal categories or targets.

What this pack helps you do

  • Configure local review periods and relevant case categories.
  • Log cases once and review exposure over time.
  • Identify development priorities and structure review conversations.
  • Create action plans with owners and follow-up dates.
  • Support periodic meetings and longitudinal development planning.
