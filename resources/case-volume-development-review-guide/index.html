Case-Volume Development Review Guide

A short, practical review for APP leaders who need to see whether case exposure, review conversations, and development follow-up are visible enough to support local planning.

Case volume is an input into a local development review. It is not proof of competence, readiness, privileging, or credentialing.

Use this guide on its own

Read each question, check the statements that describe your current process, and use the prompts to decide what deserves a local conversation. Nothing is submitted, scored, stored, or sent anywhere.

01 · Visibility

Can your team see the exposure picture?

Visibility review prompts

Discuss locally: Which categories or review-unit codes are missing, ambiguous, or changing?

02 · Cadence

Can you compare exposure over time?

Cadence review prompts

Discuss locally: What cadence would make the next review useful and sustainable?

03 · Follow-up

Can a review conversation lead to action?

Follow-up review prompts

Discuss locally: What is the smallest next action that would improve visibility or follow-through?
