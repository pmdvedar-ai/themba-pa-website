An onboarding process is not always an implementation system

Schedules, orientation expectations, preceptor practices, local approvals, and follow-up actions often live in separate places. The gap is not necessarily another policy; it is a coherent way to connect the pathway, owners, recurring conversations, open actions, and leadership decisions.

What the toolkit helps you organize

  • A locally adapted pathway for the first 30, 60, and 90 days
  • Operational owners, participants, approvers, and communication roles
  • A consistent weekly check-in and follow-up structure
  • Launch actions, dependencies, barriers, escalation, and review dates
  • Time-bounded leadership meetings with decisions and next-step ownership

How the pieces work together

  1. Frame the rollout. Use the Quick-Start Guide to confirm scope, boundaries, roles, and sequence.
  2. Build the local pathway. Define the three phases in the Pathway Template.
  3. Make ownership explicit. Assign work and decisions in the Ownership Matrix.
  4. Prepare and run the work. Move open actions to the Tracker and use the Weekly Check-In consistently.
  5. Review and adapt. Use the leadership agendas to close actions, document decisions, and approve revisions locally.
