Conduit Quality Systems Assessment

Review how your cardiac-surgery program organizes conduit-quality systems, ownership, review, and continuity. The goal is to identify one useful local planning priority.

What this assesses

This free pilot reviews program systems across seven domains. It is not a clinical assessment, diagnostic, psychometric instrument, employee-performance evaluation, benchmark, credentialing tool, or prediction of safety or outcomes.

  • Answer 21 program-level items and indicate what supports each response.
  • Review your responses before seeing a systems snapshot.
  • Receive domain priorities and bounded next planning actions immediately.
  • Your answers stay in this browser session and are not sent anywhere.
Do not enter PHI, patient or case details, employee names, protected performance information, employer identity, or confidential incident narratives.
