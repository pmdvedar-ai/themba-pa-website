Finish this guide with a completed one-page implementation plan

This guide walks you through a structured process to identify your priority barrier, select your first implementation action, and build a concrete 7-day plan — in one working session.

1. Your priority barrier identified

Use the scoring matrix to identify which of the four barriers is the most useful local starting point — considering impact, frequency, urgency, feasibility, and team readiness.

2. Your first action defined

Select one specific, bounded implementation action using the decision framework — complete enough to execute, small enough to finish.

3. Your stakeholders mapped

Identify who needs to be consulted, involved, informed, or able to approve the first step, and how to communicate it clearly.

4. Your 7-day timeline concrete

Daily actions for your first week — what you'll do each day, who you'll involve, and what you'll have accomplished by day seven.

5. Your success signal defined

Early signals that your first action is working — specific, observable checkpoints that tell you whether you're moving in the right direction.

6. Your one-page plan completed

A single-page summary you can reference, share with stakeholders, and use to communicate your first step clearly and concisely.
