APP Retention Risk Audit

This organization-level planning self-assessment reviews seven areas of retention infrastructure for surgical APP programs.

Areas Evaluated

  • First-90-Day Structure
  • Development Ownership
  • Role-Transition Recognition
  • Professional Growth and Recognition
  • Structured Check-Ins
  • Concern Follow-Through
  • Operational Continuity Awareness

What to Expect

  • The audit takes a few minutes to complete and evaluates each area with three response options.
  • Your responses remain in your current browser session only. Refreshing or closing this page will restart the audit.
  • At the end, you will receive a total score, an interpretation band, and area-specific recommendations.

This assessment is an organization-level planning self-assessment. “Risk” refers to gaps in organizational retention infrastructure—not a prediction about any individual employee. It does not assess clinical readiness or provide legal, HR, compensation, financial, or employment advice.
